4. Roles, permissions and Build48 access schedules

The service may assign roles such as Operator (OP), Secondary Administrator (SA), Company Administrator (CA) or Master Administrator. Each role has privileges within its assigned company and gate context. A role on one gate or company does not automatically grant rights elsewhere. Account identity may be retained as legitimate role assignments change.

When the Build48 User Access Schedules feature is enabled, authorised managers can create and maintain named schedules for a specific gate under Schedules → User Access Schedules, and assign a pre-existing schedule to an eligible user for that gate under Users → Access Schedule. Access periods, time zones and daylight-saving transitions may affect a user's ability to operate that gate; authorisation is checked by the server, including through supported dial-to-open functions. 24/7 (no schedule) means no additional time restriction from this feature; it does not itself grant gate rights or override revoked, expired or blocked access. Hold Open schedules are a different function controlling a gate's operating state. An administrator's changes may take effect without a separate notification to each operator. The user may ask the applicable gate administrator to review denied access.

5. Security and credentials

Users must protect passwords, authentication credentials, registered phones and recovery codes, and promptly report suspected loss or compromise through the stated support route or relevant administrator. A registered app installation may need to prove possession of cryptographic device keys. Reinstallation or changing a phone may require normal authentication, recovery-code verification or administrator reset. The Application must not silently transfer a revoked binding to a different phone. Session expiry may require signing in again; it is not itself proof that the registered phone has changed.

6. Gate operations and physical safety

The Application sends commands and displays information about gates and auxiliary outputs. Sending a command or receiving an electronic acknowledgement does not prove that a gate travelled fully open or closed. Hardware sensors may indicate a closed-position contact; they do not necessarily measure the fully-open position. Users must assess site conditions and exercise reasonable care. The responsible site owner/operator remains accountable for suitable safety systems, installation, inspection, emergency access, maintenance and local compliance. The Application is not a substitute for those protections.

7. Alerts, events and notifications

According to device configuration and authority, the Application may show gate Events History, Fail to Open (FTO), Fail to Close (FTC), gate-forced-open incidents, Tamper, Mains Fail, controller connectivity incidents, Manual and Scheduled Hold Open/release, and other technical events. Events, contacts and audit records may be associated with a particular user, or with the system where no user's action can appropriately be attributed.

Push notifications, SMS, mobile-network connections and third-party services can be delayed, disabled or unavailable. Delivery unconfirmed does not prove the recipient read the alert or is physically offline. Receipt, acknowledgement, and resolution of a fault are distinct. SA/CA recipients should not rely on a phone notification as their sole safety or emergency mechanism. Build48 testing includes preservation of valid notification registrations through routine session expiry and a visible indication when notification re-enrolment is required. These safeguards must be verified before public release.

8. Availability, monitoring and gate control

Authorised administrators may configure SA availability, gate monitoring, controller diagnostics, escalation chains and Hold Open schedules or manual holds. A scheduled hold may override or coexist with a manual request in accordance with configuration. Closing a menu, acknowledging an alert or withdrawing a manual hold does not necessarily establish physical closure. Monitoring and alert state may be delayed or subject to network conditions.
